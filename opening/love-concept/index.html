(((love is just a concept…)))

but this is all being held together somehow…

is that what matters… the how?

why not call it love?

a fundamental attraction to beingness…

existence essentially (subjectively) ‘wanting’ or ‘choosing’ to exist in some way, on some level…

but maybe not…

maybe that’s just its nature all the way down at its most measurable fundamental…

to move toward…

to tend toward togetherness amidst randomness…

maybe that’s all love really is…

our most fundamental tendency for beingness…

and being together.

in that case, I love you…

genuinely and completely…

i must… because here we are!

and i’m grateful that we’re sharing this;

that we’re being held together together.

(I love you.)
