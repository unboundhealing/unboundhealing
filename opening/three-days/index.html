it’s natural for any physical being to develop mechanisms for discerning between safety (survival, propagation, pleasure) and danger (death, pain)… it’s natural for physicality to try to continue being physical…
discernment accompanying intelligence seems to require subtly and precision as subjectively determining what constitutes “safety” or “danger” becomes increasingly complex.
so, to save some searching, thinking attempts to identify and associate whatever seems most obvious (pigment, gender, orientation, preferences) and assumes (probably) “safer with similar” as in “more likely to survive in a group” especially in a society so dependent on one another for basically everything.
perhaps some even adopt certain non-preferences in the interest of being adopted into a group deemed, for whatever subjective reasons (numbers, resources), “most likely to survive” in some “larger” or more general sense.

in other words, perhaps a mind’s instinct is to create (apparent) polarities in the interest of preserving its (apparent) container, a body…
