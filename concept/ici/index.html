Interconnected Content Initiative™ (ICI)

A playful experiment in contextual linking, discovery, and wandering.

The purpose is not organization for its own sake. The purpose is exploration.

Current Structure

  • Content Nodes — articles, reflections, questions, observations
  • Concept Nodes — pages describing ideas, experiments, or themes
  • Navigation Nodes — invitations, pathways, and points of entry

Current Nodes

  • experimenting
    First ICI experiment. A reflection on the possibility of interconnected writing.
  • zone?
    An exploration of attention, activity, and whatever might be meant by "being in the zone."
  • Almost-Origin Story
    A conversation documenting the emergence of the ICI itself.
  • Welcome in...
    An invitation and point of entry.
  • liz
    A reflection on relationship, conversation, and being seen.
  • supporting
    A donation page that somehow became an essay about coffee, gratitude, and connection.

Working Principle

Links are added when they feel natural.

Not every reference requires a link.

The goal is discovery, not completion.

The goal is connection, not categorization.

Questions

  • What happens when writing begins to reference itself?
  • What happens when readers can wander through context rather than consume isolated pieces?
  • What happens when an article becomes a doorway rather than a destination?

Status

Ongoing.

Notes

If the experiment stops being fun, stop.

If it remains fun, continue experimenting.
